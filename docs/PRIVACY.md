# FieldTrack — Privacy Policy & Data Governance

## Tracking Boundaries

### What We Track
- GPS location **only during an authorized work session** (between check-in and check-out)
- Location is collected at configurable intervals (default: 30 seconds while moving)
- Data includes: coordinates, accuracy, altitude, speed, bearing, timestamp

### What We Do NOT Track
- No location tracking outside work sessions
- No audio recording
- No covert monitoring
- No personal app usage data
- No contacts, messages, or photos

### When Tracking Stops
- Immediately after confirmed check-out
- If the employee's session is cancelled
- If the tracking service is explicitly stopped

## Employee Notification

- The Android app displays a persistent notification while tracking is active
- Employees can see: tracking status, last sync time, GPS status
- Privacy policy is accessible in-app
- Employees can end their work session per company policy

## Data Retention

- Configurable per organization (default: 90 days)
- Location points older than the retention period are eligible for deletion
- Audit logs are retained longer for compliance
- Attendance records are retained per company policy

## Data Access

- Employees can access their own attendance and location history
- Managers can access data for their assigned teams only
- Company admins can access all data within their organization
- Super admins can access all organizations
- Route history access is audited

## Privacy Contact

Each organization can configure a privacy contact (email) in Settings.
Employees can reach this contact for privacy concerns or data requests.

## Legal Considerations (India)

For India deployments, the product requires legal review of:
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) compliance
- Employment notice requirements
- Data retention and security obligations
- Employee consent for location tracking

The existence of a privacy notice does NOT constitute legal compliance.
Companies must obtain proper legal counsel before deployment.
